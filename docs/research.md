# Official research — reviewed 15 September 2026

## Validated sources and implications

1. [BOI foreign experts](https://www.boi.go.th/en/procedure_to_bring_in_foreign_experts): reachable; distinguishes Section 24 and Section 25 procedures and links separate position, placement, stay and re-entry material. The application therefore keeps position approval, personnel approval, visa validity, stay permission and work permission distinct.
2. [BOI Por.8/2568](https://www.boi.go.th/upload/content/por8_2568EN.pdf): government-hosted **unofficial English translation**. Replaces Por.3/2567; contains position/placement/extension criteria under Sections 25–26. Manufacturing entities exceeding 100 employees generally need at least 70% Thai personnel, subject to stated exceptions. Position category, age, education, experience, income, certificate conditions and exemptions matter. No eligibility decision or generic salary threshold is encoded in this app; review the applicable category in the full announcement and authoritative Thai text.
3. [Single Window](https://swe-expert.boi.go.th/main.php): live. Notices reviewed include a BOI Digital Work Permit process change effective 9 February 2026 at TIESC and staged 2026 income-evidence instructions for extensions. The page distinguishes the BOI process from ordinary Non-B handling. A maintenance notice schedules interruption from 18 September 2026 at 20:00 until service resumes 21 September at 08:30. These are dated notices; recheck the live page before acting.
4. [Single Window manual](https://swe-expert.boi.go.th/main.php?filename=sw_manual): live directory with personnel/family, positions/work locality, company/certificate, work permit and social-security sections. Use current application-specific instructions.
5. [Working in Thailand](https://www.boi.go.th/index.php?language=en&page=WorkinginThailand): reachable general reference. It is not a guarantee that all linked legacy instructions remain current.
6. [BOI older e-Expert process](https://www.boi.go.th/upload/Procedure%20to%20bring%20in%20foreign%20Expert%20via%20e-Expert%20system_75473.pdf): supports separate position/placement and work/stay actions but uses legacy system terminology and historic processing times. These timings are deliberately not used as 2026 service promises.
7. [BOI Section 25 checklist](https://www.boi.go.th/upload/06-%E0%B9%83%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%88%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%20%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%2025%20EN_42555.pdf): application-specific matrix includes company records, organization chart, technology transfer, qualifications and relationship evidence. The workbook's 16 employee document types are not an exhaustive legal checklist. Legacy upload sizes and office addresses are not adopted.
8. [Department of Employment e-WorkPermit](https://eworkpermit.doe.go.th/Home): identifies BOI-related services, including Section 62 extensions. Confirm the applicable channel with BOI/DOE; the app does not suggest that every applicant uses the same route.
9. [Immigration re-entry handbook](https://www.immigration.go.th/citizen_manual/guid_en5.pdf): re-entry is a separate procedure, with TM.8 and passport evidence. Historic TM.6 references require current confirmation. No fees have been imported into the budget.
10. [Royal Thai Consulate-General Chengdu B/IB FAQ](https://chengdu.thaiembassy.org/th/content/business-or-employment-visa-non-immigrant-b-ib?menu=68db8e1aae8d891b84392313&page=68db98b312577f346474c7c4): updated 5 August 2026. Distinguishes short business visits from regulated urgent work; visa-category changes after entry go to Immigration. This is a China-based consular reference, not a complete nationwide application checklist.

## Scope and uncertainty

Nationality alone does not identify the applicable BOI route. The promotion certificate, work category, location, family situation and responsible embassy/office must be confirmed. Installation/commissioning and short-term work must not automatically be treated as ordinary permanent placement. No deadlines, fees or approval counts are invented. The public application tracks HR actions; it does not submit, cancel, renew or legally approve any permit.
